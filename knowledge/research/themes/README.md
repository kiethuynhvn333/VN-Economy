# Research themes

Use these themes to group sources and claims. A source may touch several themes, but every claim
needs a primary theme owner.

1. `history-and-transitions` — 1975 to present; reforms, trade, productivity, and lessons.
2. `current-model-and-value-capture` — GDP, exports, domestic value added, FDI, and spillovers.
3. `foundational-capabilities` — electricity, grid, skills, capital, institutions, data, R&D.
4. `industry-clusters` — electronics, semiconductors, energy, food, software, logistics, health,
   materials, and circular economy.
5. `entrepreneurship-and-finance` — founders, talent, capital allocation, and firm depth.
6. `resilience-and-climate` — demographic, climate, geopolitical, and supply-chain risks.
7. `roadmaps-and-experiments` — no-regrets investments, staged bets, metrics, and small trials.

Do not create a new theme for every source. New themes need a reason and a note in the review log.
