# Claim extraction

An atomic claim should answer one question and preserve its evidence boundary. Record:

- `claim_id`, `source_id`, and `theme_id`;
- exact excerpt or table location;
- normalized claim;
- type: `data`, `inference`, `hypothesis`, or `recommendation`;
- geography, unit, time period, and denominator;
- source quality and confidence;
- review status and canonical owner.

Do not convert a report's conclusion into a fact merely because it is repeated in the executive
summary. `SRC-001` is intentionally not promoted yet.
